AMS-02: The Alpha Magnetic Spectrometer

At RWTH Aachen University, I led the end-to-end analysis of hundreds of billions of AMS-02 events, building reproducible data-integration, validation, and HPC workflows primarily in Python, with some C++ components. I used template fitting, Bayesian inference, and Monte Carlo simulations to measure photon flux and distinguish rare, weak signals from background.

  • Python
  • C / C++
  • Template fitting
  • Flux analysis

DATA SCIENCE EXPERIENCE02 — 04

KiDS-1000 Survey

Feb 2020 – Apr 2021

For my master's thesis at the University of Bonn, I analysed approximately 21 million galaxy observations using Python (NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib), building reproducible workflows for data validation and systematic-error correction. I applied Bayesian inference and likelihood-based models in CosmoSIS to estimate cosmological parameters, and correlation functions with E/B-mode decomposition to measure weak-lensing signals and control systematics. I also contributed statistical analysis and methodology validation to a peer-reviewed Astronomy & Astrophysics paper (2022).

  • Python
  • CosmoSIS
  • Bayesian inference
  • Weak lensing
